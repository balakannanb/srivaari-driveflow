# Srivaari DriveFlow

Build SRIVAARI AUTOMOBILES — Bike Showroom & Finance Management System (srivaari-drive-flow).

A complete, production-quality web application for a modern bike showroom and finance management system covering both cash sales and NBFC / finance purchases:

1. Enquiries & CRM: Capture customer leads, walk-ins, test-ride bookings, model preferences, and convert leads into booking records without repeated data entry.
2. Bookings & Proforma: Vehicle booking management, advance payments, model/color/variant selection, and proforma quotation generation.
3. Finance & Refinance Hub: Track NBFC loan applications, document checklists, approval/disbursement stages, and a dedicated used-bike refinance workflow.
4. Inventory Management: Vehicle stock across showroom and yard, tracking chassis/engine numbers, status, and allocation to confirmed bookings.
5. Insurance & RTO Registration: Policy issuance tracking, road tax payments, RTO documentation, and registration number assignment.
6. Deliveries & Handover: Pre-delivery inspection (PDI) checklists, gate passes, accessory fitments, and customer delivery confirmation.
7. Showroom Accounts: Daily cash/bank daybooks, customer ledger reconciliations, receipt vouchers, and monthly financial summaries.
8. Executive Dashboard & Masters: Real-time operational KPIs, branch management, master catalog of models and pricing, and user role management.
9. UI / Visual Design: Premium modern SaaS/fintech aesthetic, light-themed, crisp typography, royal blue / indigo accents, and clean card-based layouts.
